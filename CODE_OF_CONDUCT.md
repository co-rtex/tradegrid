# TradeGrid Code of Conduct

TradeGrid is a learning-focused engineering project. Contributors are expected to help create an environment where beginners and experienced developers can work together productively.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Ask questions without fear of judgment.
- Review code, not people.
- Explain unfamiliar concepts when you can.
- Give beginners room to learn and advanced contributors room to tackle difficult work.
- Credit other contributors for their work.
- Do not harass, insult, discriminate against, or deliberately exclude other members.

If a technical disagreement cannot be resolved, raise it with the Technology Chairs or VP of Technology rather than allowing it to become personal.

Club leadership may restrict participation when behavior repeatedly harms the project or its members.
